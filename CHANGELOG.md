## 26.11.1 - 2026-11-07

- Connect Field Day Commander Relay to Field Day Commander with an enrollment code to send contacts from N3FJP, N1MM Logger+, WSJT-X, JTDX, ADIF log files, and loggers that send ADIF over UDP.
- Sync contacts, edits, and deletes in both directions with N3FJP over TCP networking. Join your club's existing N3FJP network server, or let N3FJP PCs connect to the relay.
- When N3FJP PCs connect to the relay, you can keep logging without internet access. Keep the relay running while you log, since those PCs don't keep their own log.
- Receive contacts, edits, and deletes from N1MM Logger+, or collect contacts from ADIF files and UDP broadcasts. These connections send contacts to Field Day Commander only.
- Keep collected contacts on disk during a server outage and send them when the connection returns. Choose a station for contacts without a station name, or use your choice for all contacts.
- Install on 64-bit Windows 10 or later without administrator rights, with an option to start at sign-in, or run the portable version without installing. A status page shows connections and queued contacts.
